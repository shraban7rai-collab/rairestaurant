Japanese Omakase Restaurant — London

The Essence of Omakase

With just 7 tables and 12 sushi counters, RAI offers one of London’s finest yet affordable Omakase experiences. “Omakase” means “I’ll leave it up to you.”

Book a Table

Craftsmanship, Prepared in Front of You

Performed right in front of you by Chef Padam, each plate demonstrates a different level of craftsmanship. Watch as our chef expertly prepares sushi and stamps it with speciality binchotan charcoal for a charred flavour, served with 5-year-old soy sauce and rare, freshly grated Japanese wasabi.

Forget the noise of central London and enter a serene Japanese garden. Discover the captivating charm of our traditional Japanese garden, where stunning visuals meet an exceptional dining experience.

Menus

Indulge in our expertly crafted New Style and Classical Tasting Menus by Chef Padam and his exceptional team. For those who want a little bit of everything but are not quite ready for a full course, we also offer an exciting À La Carte menu, which can be enjoyed in our Japanese Garden.
